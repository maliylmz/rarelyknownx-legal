# RarelyKnownX — Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated: 11 October 2026

RarelyKnownX is a private tool used only by the owner of the RarelyKnownX YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public.

## Privacy Policy
- The app accesses only the channel owner's YouTube account, through Google OAuth.
- No data from any other user is collected, stored or shared.
- The access token is stored privately and never shared or sold.
- Access can be revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Terms of Service
- Only the channel owner may use this app. It is provided as is, without warranty.
- Use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

## Contact
Use the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
